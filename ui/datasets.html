Field registry

Every dataset carries a registry of its own fields. The rule builder populates its dropdowns from here, so the engine never hardcodes a counterparty's vocabulary. Role gives the engine semantics without names: it looks for the field whose role is Amount, never for a field called "Amount". Matchable is the security boundary — only these fields may appear in a rule.
